Frequently asked questions

Honest answers to everything you want to know before you start.

Is SpeakAI actually better than Duolingo?+
Duolingo teaches vocabulary and grammar through quizzes. SpeakAI teaches you to have real conversations through actual AI dialogue. They solve different problems — we solve fluency, which is what most people actually want.
Do I need any prior language knowledge?+
Absolutely zero. Your AI tutor starts at complete beginner level and builds at your exact pace. It adapts entirely to where you are right now.
How many minutes per day do I need?+
Even 15 minutes a day produces measurable fluency gains. Our most successful learners do 2-3 short sessions daily. Consistency beats intensity every time.
Can I learn multiple languages at once?+
Yes — the Fluent plan gives you full access to all 6 languages. Your progress, vocabulary, and AI memory are tracked completely separately for each language.
Is my progress saved between sessions?+
Absolutely. Your AI tutor remembers every mistake you made, every word you learned, and every concept you struggled with — building intelligently on every previous session.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?+
Yes, cancel anytime with one click from your account settings. No questions asked, no cancellation fees, no awkward retention flow.
What makes the Arabic and Urdu experience special?+
SpeakAI was built with deep roots in South Asian and Middle Eastern culture. Our Arabic and Urdu tutors use real cultural references, local expressions, and authentic context that Western apps completely miss.
Does SpeakAI work on mobile?+
Yes — SpeakAI is fully mobile responsive and works beautifully on any phone or tablet browser. A dedicated mobile app is coming in 2026.
