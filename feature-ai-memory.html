AI that remembers you

Your AI tutor builds a complete picture of your learning — every mistake, every strength, every session — and uses it to make every next session smarter.

How AI memory works

Every time you have a conversation with your SpeakAI tutor, it records what you got right, what you struggled with, which words you hesitated on, and which grammar patterns trip you up.

At the start of your next session, instead of starting from scratch like every other app, your tutor picks up exactly where you left off — focusing more on your weak areas and reinforcing your strengths.
