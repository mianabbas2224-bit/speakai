Voice-first learning

Speak directly to your AI tutor. Practice real pronunciation, get instant feedback, and build the speaking confidence that textbooks can never give you.

Why speaking out loud changes everything

The biggest problem with language learning apps is that you never actually speak. You tap, swipe, and type — but your mouth never forms the words. Then when you try to speak in real life, nothing comes out.

SpeakAI is built voice-first. You speak, your AI tutor listens and responds, and you build real speaking confidence from your very first session.

🔊
